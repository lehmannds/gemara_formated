## Goal
Move the expand/collapse toggle for collapsible groups (e.g. group, mishna+gemara)
out of the inline text flow and into a fixed gutter on the right side (the "start"
side in RTL). Text indentation must NOT be affected by group nesting at all.

## Current behaviour
- `renderer.js` creates the toggle button (`<button class="gmr-group-toggle">`)
  inline inside the line, before the `<span class="gmr-group">`.
- `prepareLines` computes hierarchy-based indentation for lines that contain a
  collapsible tag (counting expanded ancestors).
- `editor.css` styles the toggle as `display: inline-block` with a small margin.

## Desired behaviour
- The toggle appears in a **gutter column on the right** (inline-start in RTL),
  similar to the existing page gutter on the left (inline-end in RTL).
- The toggle gutter is positionally independent of the text — it does not push
  words or affect indentation.
- Grouping no longer causes any additional indentation of the actual text content.

## Files to change

### css/editor.css
1. Add a `.gmr-group-gutter` style: absolute-positioned on the inline-start side
   of `.gmr-line`, mirroring how `.gmr-page-gutter` is on inline-end.
2. Add `padding-inline-start` to `.gmr-line` to make room for the group gutter.
3. Update `.gmr-group-toggle` to remove inline-block/margin styles; the toggle
   now lives inside the gutter.
4. Nest multiple toggles (for stacked groups) vertically or with small offsets
   inside the gutter.

### js/renderer.js
1. In `renderLines` and `renderSingleLine`: instead of appending the toggle
   inline before the group span, create a gutter element
   (`<span class="gmr-group-gutter">`) and append the toggle inside it. Attach
   the gutter to the lineEl (absolute-positioned).
2. Remove `marginInlineStart` based on group hierarchy indentation. Lines should
   keep only the user-applied `>>` `<<` indent level, not the collapsible-nesting
   depth.

### js/renderer.js – prepareLines
1. For lines that contain a collapsible tag, stop computing `effectiveIndent`
   from `expandedAncestors`. Use the normal `indentLevel` (from `>>` / `<<`)
   for all lines equally.

## Acceptance criteria
- Expand/collapse toggles render in a right-side gutter, outside the text flow.
- Clicking the toggle still expands/collapses the group.
- Text indentation is identical whether a word is inside a group or not (only
  explicit `>>` / `<<` indentation applies).
- Context menu and selection on collapsed group summaries still work.
- No visual regressions on page gutter (left side in RTL).
